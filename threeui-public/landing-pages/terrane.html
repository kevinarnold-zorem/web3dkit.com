Read the ground

Pressed, folded and faulted over four billion years of patient writing — and still legible today.

Led by Dr. Ines Varela, a field geologist and cartographer, every route is plotted along an exposure worth reading — a road cut, a sea cliff, a fault scarp.

We travel with local surveyors, sleep close to the outcrop, and keep the group small enough to gather round a single hand lens. Whether it is a dawn traverse of a rift wall or an afternoon spent on one metre of shale, the aim is the same: to leave able to read what you are standing on.
