03 The craft · 製法

Made in Wakayama · 和歌山県

SLOW HEAT. FOUR DAYS.

Every batch is 40 bottles. No extract, no colouring, no shortcuts—just time, temperature and attention. The way a small kura would do it.

  1. 01

    Roast 焙煎

    Garlic and sesame are roasted low in cast iron until sweet and just golden.

    115°C · 6 hours
  2. 02

    Infuse 抽出

    Rice oil is warmed with three chilies—takanotsume, sansho and a smoked Kishū pepper.

    90°C · 90 minutes
  3. 03

    Rest 熟成

    The blend rests in ceramic for three days so the heat rounds and the aroma settles.

    Dark · 72 hours
  4. 04

    Bottle 瓶詰

    Hand-filled, hand-labelled, batch-numbered. Best within 6 months of opening.

    Batch 01 · 40 bottles
