Good to know

YOUR
QUESTIONS

Everything you need before bringing Aurello to your next golden hour.

Is Aurello ready to drink?

Yes. Every can is fully mixed and sparkling. Enjoy it chilled straight from the can, or pour it over ice for the full spritz ritual.

How should I serve it?

Chill well, pour into a large wine glass filled with ice and garnish with a fresh orange slice. No measuring or additional ingredients required.

How much alcohol is in each can?

Each 330 ml Aurello can contains 5% alcohol by volume. Please enjoy responsibly.

Can the cans be recycled?

Yes. The aluminium cans are recyclable wherever local collection accepts beverage cans. Check the rules in your area.
